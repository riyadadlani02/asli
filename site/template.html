03where it happens

Four modes. One keeps the number.

Same audio, same hesitation, same gate. The recogniser hears all seven digits every time — but only verbatim returns them. The romanising modes drop one, and on shorter utterances they lose the number entirely.

04the smoking gun

The partial is complete.
The final is not.

Every message on the socket, no early exit. The partials carry the whole utterance. The final that lands 105 ms later has the digits removed — while reporting end_s 3.607, claiming to cover the entire turn.
