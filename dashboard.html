The cliff is real, and it sits just above the default

Twelve utterances streamed in real time at each gate setting. The curve is flat at 1.00 across every value up to and including the 500 ms default, then falls to zero. Raising the gate to 700 ms removes the effect entirely at this hesitation length.

tracepartial vs final

The recogniser hears it. The final transcript drops it.

Reading every message on the socket with no early exit: the streaming partials contain the complete, correct utterance. The final that arrives 105 ms later has the digits removed — while reporting end_s 3.607, claiming to cover the whole turn.
